# Environment asset licences

Every 3D environment asset in Emberline's open mission zone, with its source and
licence. All of it is **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain): commercial use is
permitted and attribution is not required by any of these licences. Credit is
given anyway, in `Assets/Art/CREDITS.txt`, because these creators earned it.

The per-pack `License.txt` shipped by each creator is kept next to that pack's
models under `Assets/Art/Environments/`, unmodified.

character packs.

0.79 m. Only the props are used; the buildings are miniatures, not walkable
architecture, so Kenney's Fantasy Town Kit provides the buildings instead.

## Assets deliberately NOT used

Recorded so the same ground is not searched twice.

| Source | Why not |
|---|---|
| **Poly Haven** 3D models | Photoreal PBR scans. Style mismatch against flat-shaded low-poly characters. Their HDRIs remain viable for a skybox. |
| **KayKit City Builder Bits** | CC0 and directly fetchable, but modern city content — cars, traffic lights, fire hydrants. |
| **Quaternius** packs | CC0 and a good style match, but every download routes through a Google Drive folder with no scriptable archive URL. |
| **KayKit Forest Nature Pack** | CC0, same artist as Emberline's characters, and the best style match found — but itch.io only, behind a CSRF-token POST flow. Worth fetching by hand if the forest is ever revisited. |
| **Poly Pizza** | Hosts CC0 Quaternius torii gates and temples, and the download URLs are stable, but its Terms of Service forbid automated download. Manual picks only. |
| **YumeForge Japanese Rural Village** | Has the torii and stone lanterns, but ships a bespoke EULA rather than a CC licence, and its cozy hand-painted direction is tonally opposite to Emberline. |
| **OpenGameArt "Shrine, Shinto, Japan"** | LGPL / CC-BY-SA — share-alike is a viral obligation on shipped art. |
| **Sketchfab** | Downloads require an account login. |

### The East-Asian architecture gap

No CC0, low-poly, modular, directly-downloadable Japanese architecture pack
exists. This was searched across Kenney, KayKit, Quaternius, OpenGameArt and
Poly Pizza and the gap is real.

Emberline's answer is to **build its own** rather than import one model from a
foreign palette and break the single-atlas coherence that makes the valley read
as one world. `EmberZoneDressing.Torii` and `.Shrine` assemble the gates and the
wayshrine from Fantasy Town timber — uprights, a lintel, a ridge beam, a raised
platform under a high roof. That keeps the whole zone on one material and gives
the game its own silhouette instead of a borrowed one.
