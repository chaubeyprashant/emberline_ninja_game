## THE PROMPT

You are writing for **Emberline: 3D Ninja Action**, a mobile 3D action game. Keep to
the facts below. Tone is serious, restrained and cinematic: emotionally brutal rather
than gory, no comedy, no anime exaggeration, no arcade celebration. The hero is not a
straightforward hero and the villain is not a cartoon. The theme is that revenge
destroys the person seeking it, and the player should slowly notice the hero
becoming like the people he hates, right up until he chooses not to.

### The world the hero moves through

A dark, feudal-Japan-flavoured mountain country of lanterns, rain and snow. At its
heart is **Yorune**, a mountain village that burned ten years ago. North of it the
roads, forests, marshes and mountains belong to the warlord **Kagehira**, called
**Kagachi, the Serpent**. His banner is a serpent, and it hangs every hundred paces
on the roads he owns.

The journey, in the order the player walks it: the **ruins** of Yorune, the
**Lantern Road** and its night camps, a **castle checkpoint** and toll post, the
**black pines** and bamboo of a silent forest, a fog-bound **marsh** and graveyard,
a **drowned temple** under the water, the **lantern network** of broken villages,
records houses and signal towers, **Goro's mountain territory** of prison camps and
an execution ground, the **reed village** of people who chose the marsh over the
warlord, the **rain-soaked rooftops** and garrison town of Jin Kurogane, the
**frozen mountain road** and an avalanche, the **Iron Fortress**, the **Warlord's
Hall**, the ancient **Seal chamber** beneath it, the **burning fortress** and the
**summit**, and finally **Yorune at dawn**.

Everything in this world is hostile to a lone man: soldiers who know his family
name before he speaks, assassins sent for him specifically, a forest that measures
him, a marsh whose water rises while he stands in it, a mountain that kills the
enemy's own men, and things in the fog that were there before any warlord.

### The hero

**Renzo Kurogawa**, the last lantern-bearer of a dying ninja order. Son of Yorune's
swordmaster. He was nine the night the village burned. He is quiet, controlled and
damaged. He is not a revenge machine; he dismantles Kagehira's empire piece by piece
so that nobody else loses what he lost, and the story watches what that costs him.

His family: his **father**, the swordmaster, one of two keepers of the Black Seal,
who refused Kagehira and died holding a door. His **mother**, who carried the Seal
out of the burning village, gave it to a child, led the villagers to the ridge and
turned back for the last child. His sister **Aiko**, who hid the Seal under the
shrine floor, never told anyone, and was taken by raiders. She has been Kagehira's
prisoner for ten years. She left a red thread bracelet behind; Renzo ties one of her
beads into his own wrist and wears it to the end.

His motto comes from Aiko, as a child, in the opening: **"When you're near, nothing
bad can happen."** It is the emotional spine of the game. Later the memory returns
it as a plea: **"Ren, be near."** The counter-motto is his father's last message:
**"Whatever it is you are angry about when you hear this, be less."**

What he wants changes as he goes. Missions 1 to 30: to learn who burned Yorune and
whether Aiko survived. Missions 31 to 70: to find Aiko and reach the man who sold
the village. Missions 71 to 100: to reach Kagehira. What he is afraid of is stated
by his rival Jin: **"If you reach Kagehira, you may become him." "I am not him."
"Neither was he."**

His inner state across the campaign, as authored: 1–20 confused, 21–40 angry,
41–60 obsessed, 61–80 consumed, 81–100 changed.

### The opening (played before mission 1, title THE LAST EMBER)

Yorune at peace. Father to the boy: "Again. Slower. The blade is not in a hurry."
Aiko to her brother: "When you're near, nothing bad can happen." Then fire. Kagehira
to the father: "The Black Seal. Say where it is, and this stops." The father: "You
will burn it all either way." The village in ruin. The boy: "…Father."

### The thing everyone is fighting over

The **Black Seal**. For the first half of the game it is a rumour, then a hunt, then
evidence. The truth, found rather than told: it is not a weapon. It is a lock with
three keys, made by Renzo's father, and it controls the mountain's water and every
village that drinks from it. Kagehira wants it because with it he is order, and
without it he is a man with an army, and armies end. Only a Kurogawa can open the
door. That is why he burned Yorune, why he kept Aiko for ten years asking one
question, and why he has been expecting Renzo for months.

### The obstacles, in the order he meets them

- **The Convoy Captain**, keeper of the Lantern Road. His saddlebag holds the first
  mention of the Black Seal.
- **Goro, the Toll-Captain.** A giant, bare-chested and firelit. The first named
  enemy. He was the young raider captain at the door of Renzo's father's house.
  Beaten at mission 5, where he lets slip that Aiko may have survived. Hunts Renzo
  personally at 38, where he cannot be beaten, only outrun. Dies on his own gate at
  40: "The marsh. She's under it."
- **The Scavenger King**, who made a second burned village his own.
- **The Three Blades**, three assassins who fight as one, sent for Renzo by name.
- **The Pale Shade**, what the marsh kept. Not Kagehira's; it was there before him.
  It hunts Renzo through the forest at 21 and cannot be killed there. Dies at 30
  and names where Aiko was moved.
- **The Drowned Guardian**, set beneath the temple by Renzo's own father to keep the
  third key from anyone, including his own children. It holds back when Renzo bleeds
  and hits harder when he does not.
- **Jin Kurogane, the Storm Blade.** Kagehira's finest officer, from Yorune himself.
  He drew Kagehira the map, then tried to stop what came, failed, and quit. He is
  the only enemy who fights with honour. He beats Renzo twice and spares him twice.
  He confesses: "I gave him the map. I did not give him the village. He took that
  himself." He warns: "If you reach Kagehira, you may become him." He dies in the
  rain at 70, half a broken mask in his hand, telling Renzo that Aiko is alive in
  the mountain fortress: "Do not become him."
- **The Iron Guard**, Kagehira's shield. They were Goro's men once. They wear
  Yorune steel.
- **Commander Hoshu**, the last door. Jin's champion at 66, where he yields; the
  final commander at the inner gate at 79, where he dies.
- **Kagehira / Kagachi.** He and Renzo's father carried the Seal together, two
  keepers. "One kept faith with the villages. The other kept faith with the future.
  One of us was right, boy." "He chose people." "Let us find out which."

### How the story goes: three acts, ten chapters

**ACT I, THE RETURN (missions 1–30).**
*Chapter 1, Ashes of Yorune (1–10):* Renzo comes home to a village that no longer
exists and learns the people who burned it never left. He follows a map, a convoy,
a camp, a gate, and beats Goro, whose ledger carries a name he has not heard in ten
years: AIKO KUROGAWA. He follows her trail into the pines, finds his father's mark
cut into a tree, and in a drowned temple learns the Seal is bound to his own family.
*Chapter 2, The Lantern Network (11–20):* Kagehira is not hunting with a raiding
party; he is building an army and buying steel from every province. A survivor
remembers Aiko alive after the fire. Decoded orders read "Find the daughter. She
knows where he hid it." Prisoner rolls confirm she was alive six years ago. As the
second signal tower burns, its last message reads: KUROGAWA IS COMING. They know
his name now.
*Chapter 3, The Silent Forest (21–30):* Renzo is the prey. Something pale in the
trees measures him and lets him live. Assassins kill one of Kagehira's own officers
for refusing to take a child north. The Three Blades come for Renzo by name. Red
thread tied at a child's height leads to a bead from Aiko's bracelet; she was leaving
a trail. A staged rescue is a trap. The forest closes, and the Pale Shade dies
telling him she was moved to the toll-captain's country.

**ACT II, THE HUNT (31–70).**
*Chapter 4, Goro's Territory (31–40):* War. Goro has emptied the valley into pens.
Renzo frees wagons, burns a camp, stops an execution (everyone on the platform
lives; none of them is her), breaks a formation, rescues the blacksmith who marked
every blade he was forced to make, and holds a village against a siege. Goro comes
himself, hunts Renzo to the ridge, and lets him run to fight on his own ground.
Renzo burns the mountain gate open and kills him.
*Chapter 5, Into the Marsh (41–50):* Isolation. Rising water, voices in the fog,
a sunken camp whose records say the "daughter" was moved to the temple "for the
key". Aiko is not a prisoner; she is a lock. Assassins with a Kurogane crest. A reed
village and an old guide who knew his father. Under the water, a Kurogawa chamber:
the Seal is a lock with three keys, his father made the keys, and Renzo takes the
first. "He hid it and they burned the village for it. Father. What did you do."
*Chapter 6, The Drowned Temple (51–60):* His father's journal, and the memories it
unlocks. Renzo walks Yorune's last ordinary evening, then the burning, then fights
as his father at the door (the raider captain is a young Goro), then plays his
mother's escape, then plays Aiko herself, with no sword, hiding the Seal under the
shrine floor before a hand closes over her mouth. Back in the present he finds her
cell, reads her handwriting for the first time in ten years, takes the second key,
kills the Drowned Guardian, and finds beneath it not a key but a message. Kagehira
came for the Seal, not for them. "The man who drew him the map is called Kurogane.
He was my friend." Renzo stops looking for answers. He is looking for Kurogane.
*Chapter 7, Kurogane (61–70):* Jin cannot be beaten at first, then chases Renzo
across the rooftops, then beats him and lets him live: "Go home. There is nothing
up this mountain but me." Renzo digs into Jin's past, finds a portrait of Jin ten
years younger standing behind his father, fights Jin's men ("He tried to stop it"),
beats his champion, and in Jin's burned house finds half a mask. The confession, the
warning, and the duel neither can walk away from. Jin dies honourably and tells him
where Aiko is.

**ACT III, THE END (71–100).**
*Chapter 8, The Iron Fortress (71–80):* The climb through snow. Guns, an avalanche,
the outer wall held by a commander in Yorune steel, a silent drain into the inner
fortress, a prison tower where every freed cell is a voice that might be hers and
none is, an empty cell still warm, the Iron Guard, the inner gate. The throne hall
is full of every lantern from the drowned road, and Kagehira is gone. Behind the
throne: Aiko. Standing. Older. Alive.
*Chapter 9, The Black Seal (81–90):* "You came." "I said I would." "You were nine."
Through a night of waves she tells it: he asked every day for ten years, and it is
under the shrine floor; he never thought to look at home. Renzo gets her out of the
fortress. Half the army mutinies; they followed Kagehira for conquest, not a seal,
and they stand down at the sight of her. The Seal's door carries their father's
mark and a message for both of them: "The Seal is not a weapon. Do not let anyone
make it one. Do not make it one yourselves." The chamber's carvings say what it is.
Kagehira arrives, talks, and every sentence is a cut: "I burned Yorune for every
well." The fortress holds against his army; he goes around it, opens the door with
Aiko's blood on the key, and climbs.
*Chapter 10, The Serpent's End (91–100):* The fortress burns behind him. The last
army, the summit road, the final guard, an ambush in the fog where Kagehira takes
Aiko and says "Come alone." Renzo climbs alone. Kagehira tells him the final truth
about the two keepers. In the opened chamber, with the water rising, four phases:
swordsman, warlord, collapsing arena, exhausted duel. Kagehira on his knees. Renzo
raises the sword. Aiko: "Don't become like them." He lowers it. Kagehira's last
word is "…Weak." He dies anyway, from his knees, on his own last attack. Renzo did
not become him. Then dawn over Yorune: "Where will you go?" "Home." "There is no
home." "Then we'll build one."

### Why he keeps going: the rule of the campaign

Every mission ends on the reason the next one begins. No mission is a menu item.
Each is authored as quiet, tension, discovery, combat, quiet, escalation, climax,
resolution. What Renzo achieves in a mission is never just a body count: it is a
lead, a name, a key, a person saved, or a truth he did not want.

### What he does and what he gets, mission by mission

91. **The Burning Fortress** (night, fire). Gets Aiko out as the fortress falls in the order the fire chooses. Learns Kagehira set the fire himself, burning the bridge behind him.
92. **The Last Army** (snow). Fights through what remains of Kagehira's army until the mutineers break the line from behind. The army fights for pay; the mutineers fight for Aiko. It shows.
93. **The Summit Road** (snow, fog). Endures the climb past the guards who could not keep up. Learns Kagehira's own guard died on this road and he climbed on alone.
94. **The Final Guard** (snow gate). Beats Kagehira's strongest, told to make sure Renzo did not arrive whole. The gate opens on an empty summit.
95. **The Serpent's Shadow** (fog, night). Survives Kagehira's ambush from the fog; Kagehira chooses not to make the cut at his back. "Alone. Come alone." Aiko is gone from his side.
96. **No Way Back** (night stair). Fights alone through the last shades and the last men. Learns the shades were Kagehira's all along. A voice he knows waits inside the chamber door.
97. **Father and Son** (night chamber). Hears that his father and Kagehira were two keepers of one Seal. "He chose people." "Let us find out which." Refuses the offer; fights the guard.
98. **The Black Seal** (opened chamber, water rising). Enters the place he has only seen carved. Kagehira waits at the Seal with Aiko beside him, unbound. She has not run.
99. **Kagachi** (chamber, night). Beats Kagehira through four phases. Raises the sword over him. Aiko: "Don't become like them." Lowers it. Kagehira dies on his own last attack. Renzo did not become him.
100. **Emberline** (Yorune at dawn, no combat). Walks out with Aiko, who sees the red thread on his wrist. "Where will you go?" "Home." "There is no home." "Then we'll build one." Fade to black.

### After the campaign

Finishing mission 100 unlocks New Game+ (harder enemies, altered compositions,
nightmare duels), the full nine-opponent duel roster (Goro, the Pale Shade, the
Three Blades, the Convoy Captain, the Drowned Guardian, the Iron Guard, Commander
Hoshu, Jin Kurogane, Kagachi), and the Infinite March, an endless road through the
campaign's environments.

### The vocabulary of play, for when the writing needs it

- **Endure**: a mission against a foe you cannot beat yet. The clock ends it, not a corpse. Used for the Pale Shade, Goro, Jin and Kagehira.
- **Execution**: a staggered, nearly dead enemy can be finished in a locked, framed, decisive animation. At mission 99 the execution is offered and refused.
- **Perfect dodge**: a dodge on the exact frame slows time and opens a counter.
- **Telegraph**: the ring an enemy shows while winding up. White is a normal attack; red is a dash or a desperation move.
- **Sen and Gates**: Renzo's energy, flowing through five gates. Surging cracks a gate and lowers his ceiling until he rests. Attrition, not a regenerating bar.
- **Ember Shards**: earned by mission rank and feats, spent on the skill tree. **Ryo**: earned by marching, spent at the Forge on weapons and cosmetics.
- **Feats**: achievements evaluated at mission end. **Duels**: one opponent, full health, distinct kit, a name card and a taunt.
- **Optional objectives**: no alarm, save every prisoner, no civilian deaths, a silent kill, under time. Meaningful, not mandatory.

### Rules for anyone writing in this world

Keep Renzo quiet; he speaks in short sentences and rarely first. Never let him
celebrate a kill. Let enemies talk more than he does. Kagehira is never a monster
and never wrong by his own lights. Jin is the mirror, not the villain. Aiko is not a
damsel; she hid the Seal at nine and kept the secret for ten years under daily
questioning. The red thread is the only warm colour that matters. End every scene on
the reason the next one begins.
