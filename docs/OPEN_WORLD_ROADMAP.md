# Emberline — open-world implementation roadmap

Date: 2026-09-15. Follows `docs/OPEN_WORLD_AUDIT.md`. This is the order of work
to turn the current valley-plus-campaign into a premium open-world shinobi game
for Android, in vertical slices, without regressing the 100-mission campaign.

Ground rules that every phase inherits:

1. **Canon is read-only.** `CampaignTable.cs`, `docs/CAMPAIGN.md`,
   `docs/VILLAINS.md`, the companions, the nine camps and five villages, Sen and
   Gates, the weapon roster and the emotional arc are the spec. New systems
   serve them. No mechanic is added that a shinobi in Yorune would not have.
2. **Everything stays code-generated.** New scenes, prefabs and data are produced
   by `Assets/Editor/*`; nothing is hand-edited in the editor. New MonoBehaviours
   live in files named after them (`AssertNoMissingScripts` stays).
3. **Every phase closes the same way**: `SetupScenes` compiles with zero
   `error CS`; `EmberCampaignBatch.All` passes; the playthrough bot clears the
   touched missions; the relevant snapshot tool is re-rendered and looked at;
   the perf overlay numbers are recorded in `docs/PERF_BASELINE.md`; the
   changelog gets a section. A phase that breaks the campaign does not merge.
4. **Medium difficulty stays 1.0 on every axis.**
5. **Mobile first**: 30 fps floor on the Galaxy A33 at tier 1, 60 on tier 2+.
   Any system that cannot meet that on the reference device is redesigned, not
   shipped.

Sizes: S = days, M = one to two weeks, L = three to five weeks of focused work.
The estimates assume the existing batch/bot/snapshot loop and no human QA until
Phase 10.

**URP decision.** The brief asks for URP; the project is Built-in with six
hand-written CG shaders (~630 lines), one `OnRenderImage` grade and all
materials generated by code. The migration is bounded (port six shaders to URP
HLSL, grade → `ScriptableRendererFeature`, quality tiers → URP assets, overlay
cameras for the three extra canvases) and buys the SRP Batcher for ~2,000
static props, Forward+ for lantern lights, integrated post-processing and render
scale. It is scheduled as the **first slice of Phase 8**, not here, because the
draw-call wins the valley needs first (occlusion, LOD, instancing, distance
culling) are available in Built-in and are Phase 9 work that transfers
unchanged; switching pipelines before the world is stable would double every
visual verification in Phases 2-7. Until Phase 8, new shaders are written once
in a form that ports (no surface shaders, no `OnRenderImage`).

Exit: baseline recorded; tests green; save round-trips a migrated profile;
campaign bot 1-100 unchanged.

## Phase 2 — Player movement and traversal (L) — first slice landed 2026-09-16

Landed: smoothed motor with four gaits and swimming, `TraversalProbe`
(vault / mantle / grab / hang off real colliders, edit-mode course check),
slide, landing roll, wall-run on a layer mask with air grab, camera pitch band
/ recentre / lead / profiles / terrain floor, contextual JUMP button, safe
area, stick dead zone, gait and crouch blend trees with the IK pass and foot IK,
crossfaded AI pose changes. See `docs/TRAVERSAL.md`. Still open from the list
below: real Mixamo takes for the thirteen traversal poses (fallbacks ship),
rope and zip lines, hand IK on ledges, the control customisation panel, the
`ZoneControls` retirement (Phase 5), and the human feel session.

Goal: Renzo moves like a shinobi on real ground — fluid, no popping, no sliding.

- **Motor**: keep `CharacterController`; add acceleration/deceleration curves,
  slope handling and step logic, camera-relative input with a dead zone, a
  velocity-driven 2D locomotion blend tree in `SkeletalRig` (walk / run /
  sprint / crouch-walk), turn blending, crossfaded pose changes instead of
  speed-0 scrubs. Root motion only for mantles, vaults and takedowns.
- **Gaits**: stealth walk, walk, run, sprint (stamina-free; Sen stays the only
  resource), crouch, slide from sprint, roll as an animated dodge distinct from
  Flicker (Flicker stays the i-frame air dash).
- **Traversal module** (`Player/Traversal/`): capsule-sweep probe that
  classifies step / vault / mantle / climb-up / ledge-hang heights from real
  colliders, replacing the marker-based vault; ledge grab and shimmy; wall-run
  rebuilt on the probe with layer masks; roof and wall movement on the village
  houses and camp palisade (colliders already authored per prop). Rope/zip lines
  as authored `TraversalLine` props between towers and roofs. Swimming in the
  river as a surface-swim gait (water plane already exists) — Renzo can cross
  the river, not dive.
- **Animation**: Mixamo clips for the new gaits imported through
  `EmberCharacterFactory`; IK pass for feet on slopes and hands on ledges
  (`OnAnimatorIK`), gated by tier.
- **Camera** (`CameraRig`): real pitch, terrain-height floor, auto-recenter
  behind movement, explore/stealth/combat profiles as a ScriptableObject,
  framing dead-zone, velocity look-ahead, closer-not-clip in corridors.
- **Controls**: `TouchControls` prefab (safe-area anchored, one canvas config
  shared by all five canvases), contextual action button (Jump → Vault → Climb
  → Interact by probe result), sprint on stick push-through, customisation
  panel (size, opacity, position, left-hand, sensitivity, invert), 20:9 and 4:3
  layouts. `ZoneControls` deleted.

Verify: a new `EmberTraversalCourse` editor scenario (stairs, 0.5/1.2/2.2/3 m
walls, ledge, roof run, rope, river) driven by the bot; snapshot the course;
device session for feel. Exit: campaign bot unchanged; no pose pops in the
lineup snapshot; p95 unchanged from baseline.

## Phase 3 — Combat (M)

Goal: keep the commitment model, close the gaps the prior audits left open.

- Split `CombatController` into `SwingResolver`, `TargetSelector`,
  `PlayerGuard`, `Core/TimeDip`, `PlayerWeaponVisuals`; `PoisonPuddle` to its
  own file. `IDamageable`/`ITarget` so villagers, props and future allies share
  one hit path.
- Block as its own input (hold), parry as the tight start of the block, perfect
  parry unchanged; directional defence wired (`WithinGuard`, `LastSwingDirection`
  → enemy hit reactions and player reactions); weapon clash (`CombatHitResolver`
  as COMBAT_3 specified); guard break, finisher and air attack unchanged but
  driven by the resolver; `hitStop` per attack read from data.
- Ranged: bow as a real mode (aim camera, draw, arc, headshot on unaware),
  kunai/shuriken as quick throws, smoke bomb unchanged. All through the
  slot-based loadout below.
- Equipment: slot-based `Loadout` (katana, secondary, ranged, tool, armour,
  accessory) with a stat-aggregation pass so `ApplyWeapon` can run mid-mission;
  `WeaponTrait[]` replaces the three bools; armour and accessory
  ScriptableObjects with meaningful modifiers (noise, visibility, poise, Sen).
- Stealth attack: rear-arc takedown with its own animation and a body left
  behind; sleeping/unaware variants.
- Enemy hit reactions by direction and weight; stagger readability; camera
  impact already good.

Verify: `EmberCombatCheck`, combat scenarios, duel integrity, difficulty
scalars all green; a device feel session on parry, dodge, block. Exit: Medium
numbers untouched; nine duels unchanged.

## Phase 4 — Stealth and enemy AI (L)

Goal: a real shinobi stealth loop and enemies that behave like a garrison.

- **Navigation**: add `com.unity.ai.navigation`, bake a NavMesh on the valley in
  the generator, `NavMeshAgent` for enemies and NPCs with the existing
  `MovementStyle` steering as local behaviour on top; enemy-enemy separation;
  attacks abort when blocked.
- **EnemyBrain split**: `EnemyPerception`, `EnemyMotor`, `EnemyAttackResolver`,
  `EnemyDefence`, per-boss scripts, `TelegraphRing`; `AiState` becomes the
  authoritative top-level machine: IDLE → PATROL → SUSPICIOUS → SEARCHING →
  ALERT → COMBAT → RETURN_TO_PATROL, with the existing combat FSM nested under
  COMBAT.
- **Perception**: per-def vision range/cone, real hearing from all noise
  sources (footsteps by gait and surface, kunai impacts, thrown stones, doors,
  combat), light volumes from lanterns and fires with darkness advantage, tall
  grass and hide spots (haystack, under-floor, water) as `Concealment` volumes,
  suspicion meter with local propagation (shout radius, LOS, alarm bells and
  horns as props that call the camp), search that sweeps last-known position
  and nearby cover, de-escalation back to patrol, corpse discovery raising
  alert.
- **Patrol**: `PatrolRoute` waypoints with wait/look actions and guard posts,
  authored per camp/village in the generator; night rosters differ from day.
- **Archetypes**: line soldier (ashigaru, spear/shield, directional block),
  archer on towers, samurai (blocks, punishes), heavy, assassin, true ninja kit
  (smoke, wall traversal, kunai), elite guard at posts, captain with orders
  (rally, send runner to alarm); the eight named stat clones each get one
  unique attack or profile.
- **Squad**: planned surround slots, flank assignment, retreat/regroup,
  cover use for archers, group search patterns.
- **HUD**: detection indicator, per-enemy awareness pips, vision cones
  visible in stealth camera.
- **Friendly combat brain** (companions, militia): the one genuinely new
  system the design doc names; target model generalised from "the player" to a
  side. Tsuru's covering fire is the first user.

Verify: `EmberAiCheck` extended with stealth scenarios (patrol undetected,
noise lures, search-and-return, alarm propagation). Exit: stealth missions can
be completed undetected and recovered after a slip.

## Phase 5 — Open-world systems (L) — first slice landed 2026-09-16

Landed: the 800 m region as one height function with three passes, two
gorges, a waterfall, temple hill, stronghold plateau, cave mouth, marsh,
hamlet, hideout and edge mountains; runtime chunk streaming with seeded
scatter, water and landmark dressing; thirteen named places with discovery;
the interaction system with shrines and campfires; a clock and weather that
run in explore and feed guard sight and noise; the versioned world-state
blob; explore mode from THE VALLEY on the home screen. See `docs/WORLD.md`.
Still open from the list below: missions staged at their landmarks (Phase 7
takes it), the horse, boats, a cave interior, the `World.unity` consolidation,
and people in the places (Phase 6).

Goal: the valley becomes a region, and the game lives in it.

- **World scale**: extend `ZoneTerrain` from 200 m to a region (target
  ~800 × 800 m) as a deterministic height function over authored landmark
  masks; stream in 100 m cells (terrain chunk + scatter + props generated from
  seed and an authored landmark table, pooled), never load the whole region.
  Landmarks from the design table placed on a `WorldMap`: Yorune (burned village
  as persistent state), the five villages, the nine camps, samurai outposts,
  a ninja hideout, shrine and temple, caves, a waterfall, bridges, ruins,
  watchtowers, training ground, market, fortified compound (Kagehira's
  stronghold), hidden paths and a mountain pass through the ring.
- **Hub scene**: `World.unity` replaces the three valley copies as the
  gameplay scene; it owns GameManager, HUD, missions and side content.
  Rooftop/Marsh become lighting themes on it. `Zone.unity` is retired.
- **Missions in the world**: missions staged at their real landmark instead of
  the origin (the table already names region and camp); mission plans accept
  world-anchored points; travel between missions is play (design doc S9).
- **Interaction**: one `Interactable` interface + `InteractionSystem`
  (talk, pick up, search, open, climb, hide, loot, use, enter, investigate)
  feeding the contextual button and prompt.
- **Day/night**: `TimeOfDay` clock with sunrise/day/sunset/night, driving
  light, fog, skybox, ambient life, lantern lights, patrol rosters and
  `Visibility.AmbientScale`; night is a stealth advantage without becoming
  illegible (floor on exposure).
- **Weather**: clear / cloudy / rain / storm / fog with transitions, affecting
  visibility and noise; `LevelFx` deleted.
- **Save**: player position, time, weather, discovered landmarks, cell state,
  approach taken, consequences.
- **Horse** as the lore-compatible fast travel between landmarks (a mount, not
  a vehicle system), boats on the river later.

Verify: world snapshot per landmark; streaming test drives the bot along the
road and asserts cell budgets; perf recorded per landmark. Exit: campaign
missions 1-100 pass on `World.unity`; p95 within baseline at tier 1.

## Phase 6 — NPCs, factions, dynamic events (L)

- **NPC framework**: pooled `Npc` with schedule (work / walk / eat / talk /
  sleep by time of day), carry props, reactions to Renzo (greet, avoid, flee),
  to combat (flee, hide, report to guards), return to normal; groups: villagers,
  farmers, merchants, blacksmith (Toku), monks, samurai, guards, travellers,
  children where appropriate.
- **Factions**: `Faction` ScriptableObjects (Yorune villagers, the free
  villages, Kagehira's army, Goro's toll men, scavengers, rogue shinobi,
  bandits, the marsh's dead) with reputation, territory (cells), relationships,
  patrols, outposts, leaders and unique enemy types — the design table's camps
  and villages become faction territory; village trust becomes reputation.
- **Companions** as present, speaking, losable NPCs, then fighters on the
  friendly brain (Phase 4).
- **Dynamic events**: an `EventDirector` that generates encounters from
  reusable templates weighted by faction, time and territory — patrol
  encounter, villager rescue, ambush, bandit attack, supply convoy, camp
  discovery, missing person, samurai patrol, duel challenge, hidden cache,
  ninja trial, shrine discovery. Consequences write to the world state
  (convoy stopped → camp thinner), which is the persistence the design doc asked
  for.
- **Shinobi alert system** (the "wanted" translation): a per-faction search
  level that raises patrols, closes gates and sends hunters until Renzo breaks
  line of sight and lies low.

Exit: a village at dusk reads as alive on device at tier 1 within budget.

## Phase 7 — Mission and side-content expansion (M)

- **Mission framework**: `MissionDefinition` (SO), `MissionObjective`
  (typed: reach, eliminate, assassinate, rescue, escort, protect point,
  infiltrate undetected, investigate, track, chase a fleeing target, survive,
  timed, boss), `MissionTrigger` (volume, timer, flag, damage, NPC state),
  `MissionCheckpoint` (world position, health, time, roster), `MissionReward`,
  `MissionFailCondition`; `MissionDirector` becomes the runner over these;
  existing `MissionPlan` assets migrate through the generator so all 100 keep
  passing.
- **Design table wired**: briefing approach selector (copied from the duel
  terms widget), `sets`/`reads` → save flags thinning rosters, recon persisting
  as camp intel, markers demoted behind people and landmarks with the
  accessibility toggle — items 5-10 of the design doc.
- **Side content**: contracts (assassination, rescue, investigation) from
  boards and NPCs, training and archery challenges at the training ground,
  sword duels, camp clearing with persistent state, treasure and hidden caves,
  shrine discoveries, collectibles and lore, parkour trials on roofs and
  cliffs, survival encounters at night, blacksmith upgrades through Toku.
- **Progression**: XP and levels alongside shards; five branches (COMBAT,
  STEALTH, TRAVERSAL, RANGED, SURVIVAL) grown from the twelve-node tree; Ryo
  paid in the campaign; feats and dailies rewritten against campaign ids;
  rewards of XP, Ryo, weapons, armour, skills, cosmetics, lore.
- **UI**: minimap and full map, mission menu and log, inventory and equipment,
  skill menu, detection indicator, weapon indicator, interaction prompt,
  currency and XP; text routed through `Loc.T` with a real `Resources/Loc`.

Exit: all three validators extended to side content; campaign 1-100 green.

## Phase 8 — Visual and audio polish (L)

- **URP migration** first (see Phase 1B), then post-processing per tier
  (colour grade, bloom on lanterns, vignette), render scale, Forward+ for
  lantern lights, decals for blood/scorch.
- **Environment identity**: replace Fantasy Town and Castle architecture with a
  Japanese modular kit on the same flat-shaded palette — either a licensed
  Asset Store low-poly Japanese village/temple/castle set repainted through the
  existing import pipeline, or an in-house procedural kit grown from the torii
  and shrine generators (minka houses, engawa, tiled roofs, shoji, stone
  lanterns, palisades, yagura towers, castle walls). Spec in
  `docs/ASSET_SPECIFICATIONS.md` §10. Trees: broadleaf, bamboo, pine and maple
  variants; vegetation sway in the shader.
- **Cast identity**: a coherent East-Asian cast on one silhouette language,
  replacing the Mixamo fantasy stock progressively (mooks first, named foes by
  their VILLAINS.md descriptions); textures within the class budgets.
- **Lighting**: baked GI and occlusion for static cells, one realtime shadowed
  directional, point lights on lanterns, time-of-day ambient.
- **Water, weather, wind**: a cheap river/lake shader, wet surfaces in rain,
  snow at altitude.
- **Animation**: remaining pose scrubs replaced, IK on, hit reactions by
  direction.
- **Audio**: exploration/combat/boss music per region, ten ambience beds,
  surface-queried footsteps, grass movement, wind, birds, water, rain, fire,
  village life, an AudioMixer with snapshots and ducking, spatial sources on
  the 12-slot pool; voices move out of `Resources` into compressed bundles.
- **Credits** screen surfacing the CC-BY attributions.

Exit: store screenshots can be taken from the environment (the visual audit's
stated blocker).

## Phase 9 — Performance (M)

- Bake occlusion; LOD groups (three levels) for every prop class and a
  distance-culling tier; instancing live in the shaders; static batching per
  cell; cached height grid instead of analytic `HeightAt` per query; AI tick
  LOD by distance; pooled civilians and slow zones; UI screens cached instead
  of rebuilt; the `FindObjectsByType` hitch removed; texture budgets enforced by
  `EmberAssetValidator` per class; voices and story assets out of `Resources`.
- **Quality tiers LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / ULTRA** as URP assets plus the existing
  tier dial; `PerfGovernor` unchanged.
- **Debug performance panel**: `PerfOverlay` extended with GPU time, RAM,
  batches, active NPC/enemy/VFX counts, and a perf gate in the batch run that
  fails on draw-call, tri and gc/frame ceilings.

Exit: 30 fps floor at tier 1 on the A33 across every landmark at dusk with a
village alive and eight enemies; 60 at tier 2 on a flagship.

## Risks named plainly

- **Asset identity is the long pole.** No CC0 Japanese modular kit exists
  (already searched); the choice is licence or build. Decide by Phase 5 so
  Phase 8 is not blocked.
- **Friendly combat brain** is the only genuinely new AI system; the campaign
  degrades honestly without it (allies act off-screen via existing events), so
  it must not block Phase 6.
- **Draw calls, not shaders, are the A33 ceiling.** Phase 5's streaming design
  must budget cells against the Phase 1B baseline, or Phase 9 becomes a rewrite.
- **Pipeline switch mid-project.** URP is bounded but touches every visual
  verification; it is placed where the world is stable and before the polish
  that needs it.
- **The game has never been felt by a human.** Phases 2 and 3 each end with a
  device session; the bot proves flow, not feel.
