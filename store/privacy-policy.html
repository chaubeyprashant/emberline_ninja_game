Emberline — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 August 2026 · Applies to the Android app “Emberline” (com.ergebins.emberline3d)

The short version: Emberline collects no data. None. The game runs entirely on your device, works fully offline, contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and never sends anything anywhere.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or any other data. Emberline has no user accounts, no sign-in, and makes no network connections.

Data stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of gameplay data locally on your device only — your best score, best rank, and best wave reached. This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us or anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

Emberline requests no sensitive Android permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or any other personal information on your device.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected in the first place.

Children

Because Emberline collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. The app is a single-player offline game with no chat, social features, or purchases.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes how the app handles data, this policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change. Continued absence of changes means the promise above still holds.
