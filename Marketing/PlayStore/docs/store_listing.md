## Notes before publishing

- **Character counts verified** against Play's limits (30 / 80 / 4000).
- **Emoji** are used as section markers. They render on Play and improve
  scannability, but count toward the 4,000 limit — there is headroom.
- **"soulslike"** is a genre descriptor, not a trademark, and is safe. Do not
  name competitor titles directly in the listing.
- Play's metadata policy discourages **keyword stuffing** — repeated,
  out-of-context term lists. The final paragraph is the densest part of this
  copy and is the first thing to trim if the listing is ever flagged.
