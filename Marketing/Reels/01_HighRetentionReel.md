# Emberline: High-Retention Instagram Reel (12-15s)

## 📌 Video Metadata
- **Format:** 9:16 Vertical (1080 × 1920)
- **Duration:** 12–15 seconds
- **Vibe:** Cinematic action, dark, premium mobile game, strong contrast
- **Pacing:** Fast, aggressive, cut on beats/impacts
- **Goal:** Drive account follows by showcasing real, high-skill 3D ninja combat

---

## 📝 On-Screen Text Guidelines
- **Style:** Bold, minimal, white text with a subtle dark outline/shadow for readability.
- **Placement:** Keep within the safe zone (away from Instagram UI buttons/captions on the bottom and right).
- **Rule:** Never cover the character's face, weapon, or the core combat action.

---

## 🎬 Shot List & Sequence (0.0s – 15.0s)

### 1. The Opening Hook (0.0s – 1.5s)
- **Visual:** *Immediate action.* Show Renzo entering combat or performing the strongest available combat moment right on frame 1. Emphasize the 3D environment and the close third-person camera. Both Renzo and an enemy must be clearly in frame.
- **Text:** "THIS IS A 3D NINJA GAME." (Appears immediately, disappears fast)
- **Audio:** A sudden, heavy musical boom + sword unsheathe or heavy impact sound.

### 2. The Offensive (1.5s – 4.0s)
- **Visual:** Fast, aggressive sword attack sequence. Use the absolute best-looking combat animation you have recorded. Keep the camera close enough so the action is readable.
- **Text:** "MASTER THE BLADE." 
- **Audio:** Rapid sword swings, connecting impacts, building tension in the background track.

### 3. The Defensive (4.0s – 6.5s)
- **Visual:** A perfect dodge, parry, or counter-attack. It must look satisfying and emphasize skill-based timing. (If possible, cut to a new gameplay location).
- **Text:** "TIMING MATTERS."
- **Audio:** Sharp metal clash (parry sound) or a clean whoosh (dodge), seamlessly leading into the next beat.

### 4. The Threat (6.5s – 9.5s)
- **Visual:** A powerful enemy or visually impressive boss attacking Renzo. The scale or threat of the enemy should be immediately apparent.
- **Text:** "EVERY ENEMY FIGHTS DIFFERENT."
- **Audio:** Heavy boss footsteps, roaring/grunting, or a massive attack sound effect. 

### 5. The Climax (9.5s – 12.5s)
- **Visual:** The strongest final combat moment available (e.g., boss hit, perfect parry, execution, enemy defeat, or a massive sword strike). 
- **Editing Note:** *Use a subtle speed ramp or brief slow-motion right at the apex of the hit, but ONLY if the raw footage supports it smoothly.*
- **Audio:** The music reaches its peak tension and releases. The strongest audio impact of the entire video plays on the final hit.

### 6. The Outro & CTA (12.5s – 15.0s)
- **Visual:** Fade to a clean, impressive ambient gameplay shot (Renzo standing or walking slowly). Emberline branding appears.
- **Text (Main):** "EMBERLINE" / "3D NINJA ACTION"
- **Text (Small CTA):** "FOLLOW FOR MORE"
- **Audio:** Echoing aftermath of the climax, fading tension.

---

## 🔊 Audio & Sound Design Notes
- **Music:** Intense cinematic action track. Needs a clear buildup, rhythmic beats for editing, and a massive drop/release around 10-12 seconds.
- **SFX Layering:** Do not rely solely on raw game audio. Layer in punchy sword swings, weapon impacts, heavy footsteps, enemy reactions, and subtle low-frequency whooshes to make it feel premium.

---

## ✂️ Editing Rules & Constraints
- **NO Fabrication:** Use ONLY real gameplay footage from the current build. No fake UI, characters, or VFX.
- **NO Fluff:** No unnecessary intros, no long logo animations, no developer explanations.
- **NO Anime Edits:** Do not use copyrighted audio/characters or lean into "anime edit" tropes.
- **Pacing:** Cut on attacks and impacts. Remove all dead time. Keep gameplay readable.
- **Transitions:** Avoid heavy, cheesy transitions that obscure the footage. Let the raw game shine.
